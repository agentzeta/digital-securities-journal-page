Tokenisation Has Launched. Asset Servicing Is the Next Test.

On 28 October 2026, DTCC launched its Tokenization Service across the Canton Network and private Hyperledger Besu under its December 2025 SEC no-action letter, while Broadridge reported $7.6 trillion in October Distributed Ledger Repo volume ($345B ADV). Yet monthly transaction flow is not assets under custody, and minting tokens under a Digital Omnibus Account leaves the hardest question open: what authoritative entitlement state governs dividends, elections, ex-date market claims, and cross-chain migrations?
