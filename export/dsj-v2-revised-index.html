Tokenisation Has Launched. Asset Servicing Is the Next Test.

On 28 October 2026, DTCC launched its Tokenization Service across the Canton Network (CIP-56) and private Hyperledger Besu (ERC-20) under its December 2025 SEC no-action letter, while Broadridge reported $7.6 trillion in October Distributed Ledger Repo volume ($345B ADV). In retail-facing rails, Robinhood Chain (Arbitrum Orbit L2, live 1 Jul 2026) expanded 24/7 ERC-20 Stock Tokens outside the U.S. Yet minting tokens leaves the core question open: what authoritative entitlement ledger governs dividends, elections, ex-date claims, and cross-chain migrations?

Measurement Basis & Key Rollouts

DTCC Tokenization (28 Oct 2026): 20 participants, 34 initial securities, ~$30T eligible DTC custody pool under Cede & Co. Digital Omnibus Account.

Broadridge DLR (Oct 2026): $7.6T monthly notional repo flow ($345B ADV) — velocity flow, not static AUM.

Coinbase Agentic Wallets & x402 (11 Feb 2026): AWS Nitro Enclave isolation + HTTP 402 USDC rails on Base.

Robinhood Chain & Agents (Jul–Sep 2026): Arbitrum Orbit L2 mainnet, 24/7 Stock Tokens, and HOOD Summit gated agent accounts.
